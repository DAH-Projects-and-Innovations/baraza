Take notes for this meeting?

Baraza sends an assistant into the call. It listens, then drafts the minutes for you to review.

What will happen
1An assistant named "Baraza – note taker" asks to join. You admit it.
2It tells everyone in the chat that the meeting is transcribed.
3You stop it, or it leaves when the call ends. Minutes appear in Baraza.
The extension only starts and stops the assistant. It never records audio from your browser. Open Baraza
